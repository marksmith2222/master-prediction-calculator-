No-loss scenario assumes every trade wins at your historical average winning ROI. With-losses scenario models approximately one full-stake loss per historical average number of trades per loss, resetting to your loss bet afterward. Both scenarios cap bets at available bankroll. A 20-trade sample is small and does not establish a dependable future win rate; actual results could be much worse.
